Safe by default

Point it at production without holding your breath

AI writes the query. You and your database decide what it's allowed to do.

  • Read-only unless you confirmSayQL checks every statement, and the database itself runs reads in read-only transactions.
  • Your rows stay with youWriting a query sends your question and table and column names to the AI you chose, never the data in your tables. With a local model, nothing leaves your computer at all.
  • Secrets live in your keychainDatabase passwords and AI API keys are stored in the operating system's keychain, not in files.
  • A desktop app, not a cloud serviceSayQL connects straight from your machine to your database. There's no SayQL server in between.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. SayQL Community Edition is free for personal and commercial use, and it's open source under the Apache License 2.0: the code is on GitHub. Your only cost is your AI provider's usage, which you pay to them directly with your own key. With a local model, it costs nothing at all.

Do I need to know SQL?

No. SayQL is built for people who know what they want to ask but not how to write it. Because the SQL is always in front of you, you'll pick it up along the way, and experts get a much faster editor.

Which AI does SayQL use?

Whichever you like. Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq, DeepSeek and OpenRouter are built in. Any other service with an OpenAI-compatible API works with its address. Ollama and LM Studio run models on your own computer. You pick a fast model for drafting while you type and, if you like, a stronger one for fixes and improvements, and you use your own API key.

Does my data get sent anywhere?

To write SQL, SayQL sends your question and the names of your tables and columns to the AI provider you chose. It doesn't send the rows in your tables. When a query fails, the SQL and the database's error message are sent so it can be fixed. With a local model through Ollama or LM Studio, none of this leaves your computer. SayQL connects straight from your machine to your database.

Can it break my database?

Queries run read-only by default, and the database enforces it too. A statement that could change data, such as an UPDATE or DELETE, always stops and shows you a confirmation first.

Which databases and platforms are supported?

PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, Microsoft SQL Server and Azure SQL, on macOS, Windows and Linux.

Why does my computer warn me when I first open it?

Early-access builds aren't code-signed with Apple or Microsoft yet. Signing is a paid certificate, not a safety check of the app itself. Confirm the first launch once (the steps are under the download buttons) and it opens normally from then on. Updates are signed with SayQL's own key, and the app refuses any update that doesn't match it.
