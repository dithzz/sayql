Questions

What is SayQL?

A free, open-source desktop app that turns plain English into SQL (text-to-SQL) for PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite and SQL Server. It knows your schema and runs queries read-only.

Do I need to know SQL?

No. The SQL is always in front of you, so you’ll pick it up along the way.

What does the AI see?

Your question and your table and column names. When a query fails, the SQL and the error too. Never the rows in your tables, unless you turn on agent mode for a connection, and then only what you allow for it. With Ollama or LM Studio, nothing leaves your computer.

Can it break my database?

Queries run read-only, and the database enforces it too. An UPDATE or DELETE always stops for your confirmation first.

What does it cost?

SayQL is free. You pay your AI provider for what you use, with your own key, or nothing with a local model.

Why does my computer warn me on first launch?

Early-access builds aren’t code-signed with Apple or Microsoft yet. Confirm the first launch once and it opens normally after that. Updates are signed with SayQL’s own key.
