SayQL checks for a new version when it starts. When one is available, an Update to x.y.z button appears in the status bar at the bottom. Click it to download, install and restart. Every update is signed, and SayQL refuses any update whose signature doesn't match. If the download fails, the button reads Update failed — retry.

Your version is in Settings → About.

On macOS, after an update the system may ask once whether SayQL can use its saved passwords in your keychain. Click Always Allow. All of SayQL's passwords and keys share one keychain item, so it's one prompt.
