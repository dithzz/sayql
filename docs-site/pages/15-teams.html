SayQL for teams is the governance layer for AI agents on your databases, and for the people who use them. Each agent gets its own key, only the databases you choose and only the data you allow, and every query is on record. Your company runs its own SayQL server: it holds the team's database connections, so nobody needs the database passwords, enforces what the AI may see, withholds the columns your admin marks never-send from everyone (their values never leave the server), and keeps an audit log of every query, from people and from Investigate.

Not using a team? Nothing changes. Without signing in, SayQL never contacts a team server and works exactly as before.

Set up a team server

For the person setting up SayQL for a team. You need a machine with Docker that can reach your database. On it, run:

curl -fsSL https://sayql.dev/install | sh
  • It asks for a domain, such as sayql.yourcompany.com. Point it at that machine and open ports 80 and 443, and HTTPS is set up for you. Press Enter instead to try it on that machine first.
  • It prints a one-time admin page link. Open it: you're signed in as the admin.
  • On the admin page, under Connections, add a database. The form shows the SQL to create a read-only login for it; run that first. Click Test.
  • Under People, invite your team and click Copy message: the link and what to do, ready for email or chat. The link opens SayQL, or helps them download it first (below).
  • To use the team's databases in SayQL yourself, click Open SayQL with this account on the admin page's Overview.
  • Optional: let people sign in with their Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID or Okta account instead of invite codes. On the admin page's Sign-in tab, pick your provider: it shows the redirect address to register there and what to copy back. Admins can come from a list of emails, an Okta group or an Entra app role. Sign-ins go from the browser to your provider and back to your server, never through SayQL. Needs team server 0.4.0 on HTTPS.
  • Every new server is free for 30 days, for up to 10 people. To buy, email hello@azenvoc.com; paste the licence key you receive on the admin page's Overview. Nothing restarts and nothing is lost.

Your data stays on your network: the server runs there, and the licence is checked offline. To update, run the same command again.

Govern your AI agents

For admins. Claude Code, Cursor and your own bots can query your databases through the server, over MCP, under rules you set per agent.

  • On the admin page, open Agents and click Add agent. Give it a name, tick the connections it may use, and choose what it gets back from a query: only the row count and column names, counts and summaries (the default: no row values), or all rows.
  • Copy what the page shows once: the claude mcp add command for Claude Code, or the config for Cursor and other MCP apps. They hold the agent's key, which starts with sqa_. The server keeps only a hash of it.
  • The agent can list its connections, read the schema and run read-only queries. Columns marked never send are refused and withheld on the server, and nothing can change data.
  • Every query an agent runs, or tries on a connection it isn't allowed, is in the Audit log under agent: name, with the source AI agent (gateway).
  • Change an agent's setting any time, or click Revoke: its key stops working at once.

An agent sees what you allow wherever it runs, so choose all rows only for data you're happy to give that agent.

Sign in

  • Your team admin sends you an invite link, usually starting with https://sayql.dev/join. Open it and click Open in SayQL; if you don't have SayQL yet, the page links to the download. The server and code stay in the link: sayql.dev never sees them.
  • SayQL opens Sign in to your team filled in. Check the server is your company's, then click Sign in. Nothing is sent before you do.
  • No link to click? Open Settings → Team → Sign in (or Have a team invite? Sign in in the Database Navigator) and paste the whole link, or enter the server and the code.
  • An invite code works once and expires after 7 days. Your sign-in is kept in your system keychain.
  • Sign in with your company: if your team admin turned it on, enter the server in Sign in to your team and click Sign in with your company. Your browser opens your company's sign-in page (Google, Microsoft or Okta); when you're done it asks to open SayQL, and you're signed in. No invite code needed. These sign-ins last 7 days unless your admin chose otherwise; then Signed out of your team appears and you sign in the same way again.
  • Settings → Team shows the server, your email and the team's plan and licence. Sign out ends the sign-in on the server and removes it from this computer.

Team connections

The team's connections appear in the Database Navigator under Team. They work like your own: the Ask box, the editor and Investigate all use them. A few things differ:

  • Read-only. Your team's server runs only queries that read data. A statement that would change data is refused before it reaches the database.
  • No host or password. SayQL never sees them: the schema and every query go through your team's server. Team connections can't be edited or deleted in SayQL; your team admin manages them.
  • Audited. Each query is recorded in the server's audit log with where it came from (Ask box, editor or agent) and, from the Ask box or Investigate, the question it answers. Results are never stored.

Rules set by your team admin

  • What Investigate may see. Your admin sets the most the AI may see of results on each team connection. You can choose less in Investigate settings, not more; the options above it are marked Set by your team admin.
  • Never-send columns. Columns your admin marks never send are added to the connection's list and can't be removed. Investigate refuses queries that use them, and the server leaves them out of every result (the Results panel says how many were withheld).

Team AI

Your team admin can also give the team one company AI key, with monthly limits. Then, on team connections, the Ask box, fixes, Improve and Investigate use your team's AI through your team's server, and you don't need an AI key of your own for them. Your own connections keep using your own AI provider and key.

A team without its own AI key can use SayQL AI credits: a prepaid credit key from SayQL instead of a company key (admin page → AI: provider Anthropic, base URL https://sayql.dev/api/ai). Requests then pass through SayQL's service on their way to Anthropic; SayQL keeps only the model and token counts, never the prompt or the answer. Ask hello@azenvoc.com for a credit key.

  • The Ask box shows your team's model, marked Team. Click it to see the models your admin allows; they're set by your team admin and can't be switched there.
  • Settings → Team shows who provides the AI and how much you and your team have used this month against the limits. When a limit is reached, AI requests on team connections stop until the 1st of the next month, and SayQL says so.

Privacy

When you're signed in, queries on team connections and the questions they answer go to your team's server, which your company runs, and are recorded in its audit log. Your own connections, and anything you do on them, never go there.

With team AI on, AI requests on team connections (your question, the schema and, in Investigate if allowed, masked rows) also go to your company's SayQL server, which forwards them to your company's AI provider and stores only token counts. Nothing reaches SayQL itself. Without team AI, questions on team connections go to the AI provider you chose in Settings, as on your own connections.
