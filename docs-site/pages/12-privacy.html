SayQL has no account, no server of its own and no telemetry. It sends data only to services you configure, and only when you use them:

WhenWhat's sentTo
You connect or run a queryYour login and your SQLYour database
You ask in plain EnglishYour question; table and column names, types, keys, enum values and row estimates; the current SQL for follow-ups, Explain, Fix and Improve; any skill you addYour AI provider
A query fails and SayQL fixes itThe query and the database's error, with values from your rows maskedYour AI provider
You use agent mode on a connection where you turned it onYour question, the schema, saved notes, the agent's queries and, per your setting for that connection, row counts only, counts and column summaries, or up to 20 rows per query with likely personal data maskedYour AI provider
An AI app uses SayQL over MCPThe schemas and query results it asks forThat AI app
You're signed in to a team server and use a team connectionRequests for its schema, your queries and the questions they answer, recorded in its audit logYour team's SayQL server, run by your company
Your team provides AI and you ask on a team connectionYour question, the schema, notes and, in agent mode if allowed, masked rows; only token counts are storedYour team's SayQL server, which forwards it to your company's AI provider
You first use voice inputA one-time download of the speech model (about 82 MB), checked against a fixed SHA-256. No audioHugging Face
SayQL startsThe app version and your operating system, to check for updatesGitHub (dithzz/sayql-releases)

The Ask box never sends rows from your tables to an AI provider. Agent mode can, only on connections where you turn it on; it is off by default. With a local model, nothing leaves your computer. Read the full privacy notice.

Voice input

When you say your question, the speech model (Whisper) turns it into text on your computer. Audio stays in memory only while you talk, is never saved, and is never sent anywhere, not even to your AI provider. Only the text goes on, like a typed question. Remove the model in Settings → Voice.

Feedback and the website

Feedback in the status bar opens a short form, then a pre-filled public GitHub issue in your browser. Your text is filled in, plus your SayQL version and operating system for feedback and bug reports. Nothing is sent until you post it.

This website counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, doesn't follow you across sites and stores no personal data.
